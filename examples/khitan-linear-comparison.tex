\PageTitle{Noto vs Khitan Small Linear}
These rows use the same XSR Unicode parser and fixed slots with two different
selected-font outline files. Both retain their native glyph shape and 12pt scale.
\par\bigskip
\textbf{Type A, two signs}\par
Noto:\quad {\NotoSelect\xsrKhitanText{𘬁𘬂}}\qquad
Linear:\quad {\LinearSelect\xsrKhitanText{𘬁𘬂}}\par\bigskip
\textbf{Type A, five signs (odd final row)}\par
Noto:\quad {\NotoSelect\xsrKhitanText{𘬁𘬂𘬃𘬁𘬂}}\qquad
Linear:\quad {\LinearSelect\xsrKhitanText{𘬁𘬂𘬃𘬁𘬂}}\par\bigskip
\textbf{Type A, eight signs (four rows)}\par
Noto:\quad {\NotoSelect\xsrKhitanText{𘬁𘬂𘬃𘬁𘬂𘬃𘬁𘬂}}\qquad
Linear:\quad {\LinearSelect\xsrKhitanText{𘬁𘬂𘬃𘬁𘬂𘬃𘬁𘬂}}\par\bigskip
\textbf{Type B, four signs (centered first and last)}\par
Noto:\quad {\NotoSelect\xsrKhitanText{𘬁𖿤𘬂𘬃𘬁}}\qquad
Linear:\quad {\LinearSelect\xsrKhitanText{𘬁𖿤𘬂𘬃𘬁}}\par\bigskip
\textbf{Iteration mark}\par
Noto:\quad {\NotoSelect\xsrKhitanText{𘬀𘬁𘬂}}\qquad
Linear:\quad {\LinearSelect\xsrKhitanText{𘬀𘬁𘬂}}\par\bigskip
\textbf{Missing/unidentified sign U+18CFF}\par
Linear:\quad {\LinearSelect\xsrKhitanText{𘬁𘳿}}\quad
(Noto v1.000 has no outline for this codepoint.)
\vfill
\textit{Khitan Small Linear is supplied externally and its TTF is not included in XSR.}
