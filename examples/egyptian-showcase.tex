{\large 05 / Standardized rotation and horizontal RTL}\par\medskip
The same selector means different angles for different signs. RTL reverses logical start/end.\par\medskip
\begin{comparison}\tablehead
\samplerow{VS4: 30 degrees}{A15 + FE03}{𓀒︃}
\samplerow{VS4: 25 degrees}{H6 + FE03}{𓆄︃}

\samplerow{VS4: 15 degrees}{Aa11 + FE03}{𓐙︃}
\samplerow{LTR: A1, B1, G17}{Logical order, separate quadrats}{𓀀𓁐𓅓}
\samplerow{RTL: A1, B1, G17}{Read from the right}{\xsrEgyptianDirection{rtl}𓀀𓁐𓅓}
\samplerow{RTL top-start}{Insertion is now on the right}{\xsrEgyptianDirection{rtl}𓄂𓐲𓏏}
\samplerow{RTL top-end}{Insertion is now on the left}{\xsrEgyptianDirection{rtl}𓂇𓐴𓏏}
\samplerow{RTL rotation + mirror}{F16 / FE00 / U+13440}{\xsrEgyptianDirection{rtl}𓄏︀𓑀}
\end{comparison}\vfill
{\small\color{ink}\textbf{Semantic policy}}\par\smallskip\small Unicode 17 standardized variants determine angles. Explicit NoMirror violations and unregistered NoRotate variants are errors. Registered angle-specific variants take precedence over the broad NoRotate property. Full vertical text flow is reserved; V JOINER within a quadrat remains supported.
